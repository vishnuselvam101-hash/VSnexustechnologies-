  was not verified; the masked-out lanes are never accessed by the hardware, by construction of the mask.

- Only x86-64 was tested; the non-x86 build path (scalar only) was not compiled on another architecture (a clang
  --target=aarch64 syntax check could not run: no aarch64 libc headers on this host).
